What is a Brand Guideline?

A brand guideline defines how your brand should appear consistently across different touchpoints.

ESSENTIAL: logo usage · colour palette · typography

STANDARD: essential rules plus graphic elements and visual application guidance

FULL: complete visual system with extended application and communication guidance
